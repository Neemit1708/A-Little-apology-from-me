Before anything else…

Baby,

I know I made a mistake.

And I know that what I said and the way I behaved came at a time when you were already stressed and dealing with so many things. Instead of being the person who should have given you peace, I put my own thoughts, worries and baggage onto you.

I knew you were already carrying so much, and yet I added something more to it.

For that, I am genuinely, deeply sorry.

I don't want to make excuses for myself. I don't want to explain my intentions and use them to make what happened seem smaller than it was.

Because the truth is simple—
I hurt my Baby.

And that is something I wish I could take back.

♡ · · · ♡

I know I have said this before…

This is probably the hardest part for me to admit.

I've told you before that there are things I need to understand better. I've told you that I need to know what to say, when to say it, and when my thoughts should simply stay thoughts.

And then, when the moment actually came…

I failed to do what I had said I would do.

I overthought things I didn't need to overthink. I let my mind create problems where there didn't need to be any. And instead of being your safe place, I became another reason for you to feel upset.

I am sorry, Baby. Not just for what I said, but for making you feel like you had to carry my emotions when you were already carrying enough of your own.

And then there was your application…

This one hurts me even more because you trusted me with something that mattered to you.

You gave me your work because you trusted that I would handle it carefully. You believed that when something was in my hands, I would make sure it was done properly and that I wouldn't make a careless mistake.

But I did.

I applied my mind where I didn't need to. I made an error that unfortunately couldn't be rectified, and I know that the consequences of that mistake were yours to face—not mine.

And I am truly sorry for that.

I know saying “I didn't mean to” doesn't change anything. The fact that I had good intentions doesn't make the mistake any smaller.

You trusted me, and I should have been more careful with that trust.

From now on, if even the smallest doubt comes into my mind, I will ask you. If something isn't completely clear, I will tell you. If I am unsure about even one small detail, I will not simply apply my own understanding and move ahead.

I will ask.
I will confirm.
I will make sure.

Because when you entrust something to me, I want you to feel that it is safe with me—not that you have to worry about what I might do with it.

I know I cannot undo the mistake I already made. But I can make sure I learn from it. And this is one lesson I will carry with me.

But there is something I want you to know…

Among all the things I could say to you right now, there is one thing I hope you remember.

I love you.

Not just when everything is beautiful. Not just when we're laughing. Not just when we're talking for hours.

I love you in the ordinary moments too—the random conversations, the little “good morning” and “good night” messages, the moments when you don't even realise how cute you are, and the moments when you need someone to simply listen.

And even the moments when you're angry with me.

I want to learn how to love you better, especially in the moments when I don't get things right.

What I should have been

When the world is already making you tired… I should be the person who makes things a little lighter.

When you're stressed… I should be the person who doesn't add another weight to your heart.

When you're upset… I should listen before I react.

When my mind starts overthinking… I should learn to pause.

And when I don't know what to say… sometimes I should simply hold your hand and say,

“I'm here, Baby.”

That's the person I want to become for you. Not through another beautiful promise. Through repetition. Through patience. Through the little things.

So here's my promise…

I won't promise you that I'll suddenly become perfect. Because I'm not.

But I can promise you this:

♥ I will learn.♥ I will think before I speak.♥ I will learn when to explain and when to listen.♥ I will pause when my mind starts overthinking.♥ I will own my mistakes instead of hiding behind excuses.♥ I will show you through my actions that I've learned.

Because this time, Baby,

I don't want my words to be the proof.

I want my actions to be.

I love you, Mittali.

And this time, I'll let my actions tell you the rest.

From your Pookie Pie. ❤️
